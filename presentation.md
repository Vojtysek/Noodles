# Noodles — Presentation

## Overall Design Instructions
White background throughout. Primary accent color: blue (mid-tone, clean). Rounded edges on all cards, buttons, and UI elements — generous border radius. Sans-serif font (Inter or similar). Text in dark charcoal, not pure black. Blue used for highlights, numbers, and key terms. Subtle light gray for card backgrounds or dividers. Airy, clean layout with plenty of whitespace. No dark panels, no glassmorphism.

## Slide 2 — Problem
- Renovating an apartment building takes years of planning
- Costs are unclear, subsidies confusing, neighbors skeptical
- Most projects stall before a single vote is cast

Design: White background. Large left-aligned bullet points in dark charcoal. Each point separated with generous line spacing. Optional: a soft light-gray rounded card framing the three points. No imagery — let the words carry the weight.

---

## Slide 3 — Solution
- Noodles guides housing associations from first idea to approved renovation
- One tool for calculation, persuasion, and follow-through

Design: Centered layout. "Noodles" or key phrase in blue accent. Short statement slide — large bold type, minimal elements. Thin blue rounded pill or underline as a decorative accent under the heading.

---

## Slide 4 — Calculator
- Enter your address — building data fills in automatically
- See total costs, energy savings, subsidy amount, and monthly cost per unit
- Ready in 2 minutes, no architect needed

Design: Two-column layout. Bullet points on the left, app screenshot or UI mockup on the right inside a white rounded card with a soft drop shadow. Key number (cost or savings) highlighted in blue. Light gray background on the mockup card.

---

## Slide 5 — Argumentation Phase
- Each resident archetype gets its own objections, motivations, and strategy
- AI generates tailored talking points for every type of neighbor
- Export a personalized document to hand directly to that person

Design: Show resident archetype cards as a visual element — small white rounded cards with soft shadows arranged in a subtle row or grid. Blue accent on labels or badge elements. Clean, card-based layout consistent with the app's UI.

---

## Slide 6 — Followup
- Track financing breakdown, project timeline, and real savings in one place
- See exactly when the renovation pays itself off
- Stay in control long after the meeting ends

Design: Feature a fragment of the financial chart or project timeline as a mockup inside a rounded white card with shadow. Blue for chart lines and data highlights. Light gray dividers. Two-column layout mirroring slide 4.

---

## Slide 7 — Summary
- Clear numbers to plan with confidence
- Personalized arguments to win the vote
- Ongoing tracking to see it through

Design: Three points displayed as three white rounded cards side by side, each with a small blue icon above the text. Uniform card size, equal spacing, centered on white background. Clean, structured, confident closing slide.
